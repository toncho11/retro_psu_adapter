# Compatible PSUs

The following ATX power supplies should be compatible with the VAXstation 3100 M38:

| Manufacturer | Model | Power | ATX Connector |
|---|---|---|---|
| Antec | SU-430 | 430 W | 24-pin |
| Trust | 420W | 420 W | 24-pin |
| Trust | 14995-02 | 420 W | 24-pin |
| LC-Power | LC6420H | 420 W | 24-pin |
| LC-Power | LC420H-12 | — | 24-pin |
